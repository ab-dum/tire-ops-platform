# AI on the Shop Floor

An in-house operations platform that replaced a rigid third-party ERP and spreadsheets for a tire distribution business, built with AI as the engineering partner.

**Live case study:** (https://ab-dum.github.io/tire-ops-platform/)

## What it does

- **Market-aware pricing** — pulls live competitor offers and reprices 4,000+ products across 8 sales channels through a rule engine, with a review step before anything goes live.
- **Warehouse & inventory** — barcode-scan stock counting across 150 storage locations, own stock and dropship suppliers, in a multi-language tool used daily by a 10-person team.
- **Invoicing & documents** — invoices and delivery notes generated automatically with correct VAT per country.

## Results from production use

- Per-product pricing update time: 10 minutes to under 1 minute
- ~30-40% increase in marketplace sales volume after pricing automation
- ~500 deliveries per month processed through an automated workflow
- 70% reduction in annual inventory-count effort

## Tech

Python, Flask, SQLite, REST APIs, ETL (FTP/CSV), Playwright, Linux/VPS, Docker, AI coding assistants.

All screens in the case study show example data. No real client, customer or supplier data is published, and the live system is private.
